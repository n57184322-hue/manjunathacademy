About Us

India's Most Trusted Free Exam Prep Platform

Online Kaksha was built with one belief — every Indian aspirant deserves world-class government exam preparation without paying a rupee. We provide meticulously crafted PYQs, daily quizzes, and study notes covering Banking, SSC, Railway, CDS, and AFCAT.

Founded by educators who cleared competitive exams themselves, we understand the grind. Our platform is designed to save your time, sharpen your knowledge, and maximise your selection chances.

  • ✓100% free access to PYQs and daily quizzes

  • ✓Expert faculty with years of exam experience
  • ✓Mobile-friendly platform — study anywhere, anytime
  • ✓Regular updates aligned with latest exam patterns
  • ✓Dedicated doubt-solving and community support
